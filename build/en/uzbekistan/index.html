Uzbekistan tours

Five destinations to start exploring Uzbekistan: from the modern capital to the ancient Silk Road cities and mountain landscapes.

A modern capital

Tashkent

Tashkent is the largest city in Central Asia, where wide avenues and modern districts sit next to the old town, bazaars and mosques. A convenient starting point for any trip around the country.

  • Hazrati Imam complex
  • Chorsu Bazaar
  • Tashkent Metro
Learn morearrow_forward

Historic heritage and architecture

Samarkand

Samarkand is the jewel of the Great Silk Road and the capital of Timur's empire. Turquoise domes, majolica portals and Registan Square impress even seasoned travellers.

  • Registan Square
  • Gur-e-Amir Mausoleum
  • Shah-i-Zinda Necropolis
Learn morearrow_forward

An ancient city of oriental culture

Bukhara

Bukhara is a living museum city with trading domes, caravanserais and madrasahs. Here you stroll unhurriedly along old streets and drink tea by the pool.

  • Lyabi-Hauz ensemble
  • Poi-Kalyan complex
  • Ark Fortress
Learn morearrow_forward

The Itchan Kala historic complex

Khiva

Khiva is a fortress city whose inner town, Itchan Kala, is entirely a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Clay walls, minarets and sunsets over the fortress create a fairy-tale atmosphere.

  • Itchan Kala
  • Kalta Minor Minaret
  • Juma Mosque
Learn morearrow_forward

Mountains, nature and outdoor activities

Chimgan

The Chimgan mountains are a popular getaway near Tashkent. Trekking and lakes in summer, skiing in winter, and fresh air with views of Charvak all year round.

  • Big Chimgan Peak
  • Charvak Reservoir
  • Amirsoy Resort
