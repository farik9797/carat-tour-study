Privacy policy

1. General

This policy describes how Carat Tour & Study processes the personal data of visitors to carattourandstudy.uz. By using the site and sending a request, you agree to this policy.

2. What data we receive

Your name, phone number or Telegram username, destination of interest, preferred dates and comment — only the data you enter in the request form. The site may also collect anonymised technical data (browser type, pages visited) for statistics.

3. Purpose of processing

The data is used only to contact you, select a tour and answer your request. We do not sell or share your data with third parties, except where necessary to arrange a trip with your consent or where required by law.

4. Storage and protection

Requests are forwarded to the responsible employee via secure channels and are never published. Data is kept no longer than necessary for the purposes of processing.

5. Your rights

You can ask us to correct, change or delete your data by messaging us on Telegram or by email as listed on the Contacts page.
